# THE NANNY — Pilot 01 · Kling web UI prompts
### Stage 1: character reference sheet (do this before any video)

**Settings for all three:** model **Kling Image v3.0** (or Image O1 if you want
tighter feature lock) · aspect **9:16** · resolution **2k** · generate **4
variants** each and pick one.

Do not move to video until you have three faces you are happy to look at for
sixty episodes. Re-rolling a face is cheap. Re-rolling shot 7 in episode 12
because the face drifted is not.

After picking, **save each as an Element / subject** in Kling so it can be bound
into every later shot. That binding is the whole consistency strategy.

## 3 · TEDDY MERCER — the son, 14

```
Cinematic character reference portrait, photorealistic. A 14-year-old boy with
soft features and dark tousled hair, wearing a navy hoodie, holding a small
handheld plastic puzzle toy in both hands. Calm, watchful, absorbed expression,
looking slightly away from camera as if listening to something off-screen.
Standing beside a tall window in a large minimal modern living room, soft
overcast daylight. Muted desaturated colour palette, quiet mood. Shot on 50mm
lens, shallow depth of field, upper body framing, vertical composition.
```

**Anchors:** navy hoodie · the puzzle toy · his place at the window.

> **Deliberate note on Teddy.** The prompt does not attempt to render his
> disability visually. That is a craft decision, not squeamishness: prompting a
> model for visual signifiers of intellectual disability produces caricature,
> and it is the wrong storytelling instrument anyway. Teddy reads as who he is
> through *behaviour and dialogue* — he is the one keeping count of which nights
> his mother is home. Play him as watchful and literal, and cast the performance
> in the writing, not the face.

## Stage 2 — once the three faces are locked

Bind each as a subject/Element, then generate the 11 shots with **Kling Video
v3.0 Omni** (the model that accepts video subjects) at **9:16**.

Rules that keep the episode coherent:

- **Max 3 seconds of continuous face per shot.** Drift compounds with duration.
- **Two locations only** — the kitchen/great room, and Nora's small bedroom.
- **Never generate skin-result footage.** Eleanor's rash appears once, in the
  cold open, as irritation in a held bottle-and-jaw framing. No before/after.
- **Fictional brand only** — *Maison Lelan Caviar Renewal*. The chemistry is
  real; the product must not be.
- **AI disclosure toggle ON** at YouTube upload.

Ask me for the 11 shot prompts when the faces are approved — they are written to
inherit the anchors above.
